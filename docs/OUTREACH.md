# External Pilot Outreach Kit

This kit helps maintainers and community members invite relevant participants without inflating adoption claims or spamming unrelated projects.

## Canonical Links

- Repository: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills
- Fixed release: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills/releases/tag/v0.1.1
- 15-minute pilot: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills/blob/main/docs/EXTERNAL-PILOT-QUICKSTART.md
- Questions and announcement: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills/discussions/4
- Structured evidence report: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills/issues/new?template=adoption-case.yml

## Outreach Rules

1. Post only where AI filmmaking, production tooling, continuity, evaluation, or open-source workflow research is already relevant.
2. Identify your relationship to CineOps. Maintainers should say they maintain the project.
3. Prefer one useful post over repeated unsolicited replies or direct messages.
4. Never ask participants to expose scripts, media, prompts, client identities, credentials, or private project names.
5. Welcome negative, inconclusive, and abandoned trials in the same language as successful trials.
6. Record public outreach in `adoption/outreach-log.csv`.
7. Do not count impressions, clicks, stars, forks, downloads, maintainer activity, or outreach replies as adoption.
8. If participation is compensated or otherwise incentivized, disclose that fact and do not classify it as independent without review.

## Direct Invitation

Use direct invitations only when you already have a relevant relationship or the recipient has explicitly welcomed project invitations.

> I maintain CineOps and thought its continuity/release-gate pilot might be relevant to your AI film workflow. It takes about 15 minutes and does not require sharing private creative material. No reply is needed if it is not relevant: https://github.com/loriadatj-cyber/cineops-skills/discussions/4

## Reporting

Record the date, channel, public URL, audience fit, and maintainer relationship in `adoption/outreach-log.csv`. Record responses only as outreach outcomes. An external case enters the adoption index only after an authorized, independent participant submits evidence under `docs/ADOPTION.md`.
