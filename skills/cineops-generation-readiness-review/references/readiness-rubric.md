# Generation Readiness Rubric

Critical checks:

- Continuity: identity, wardrobe, damage, props, environment, and knowledge match canon.
- Blocking: positions, facing, eyelines, paths, and interactions are physically legible.
- Performance: intention and the final emotional or physical state are observable.
- Camera: framing and movement do not contradict the action or each other.
- Transition: first and last usable frames can connect to neighboring shots.
- Generation clarity: subject, action order, and dominant change are unambiguous.

Use `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`. Attach evidence to failures and owners to unknowns.
